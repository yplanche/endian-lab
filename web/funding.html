IMPERIAL / ALL NATIONALITIES

President’s PhD Scholarships

Funding
Full tuition fees and an annual living stipend for 3.5 years; a research-consumables allowance is provided for the first three years.
Who can apply
Outstanding new PhD applicants from the UK and internationally. Academic merit, research potential and an eligible supervisor’s support are required.
Application status
For 2027/28 entry, the next published application deadlines are 11 January 2027 and 1 March 2027. Earlier departmental preparation may be needed.
How to proceed
Contact a prospective supervisor first, then apply to Imperial and follow the scholarship instructions. Supervisor availability is subject to the scheme’s rules.
Official scholarship details →

UK / ELIGIBLE COMMONWEALTH COUNTRIES

Commonwealth PhD Scholarships

Funding
Full approved tuition fees, a monthly living allowance and approved return airfare. The full-time PhD award runs for 36 months.
Who can apply
Eligible citizens or refugees and permanent residents of the Commonwealth countries listed by the provider. Academic, financial-need and development-impact requirements apply.
Application status
Closed for 2027/28 entry; the published deadline was 20 October 2026. Consult the provider for a future round.
How to proceed
Apply through the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission and an approved nominator, and separately to Imperial. Confirm that the proposed PhD can fit the funded period; costs beyond the award’s tenure are not covered.
Official scheme and funding terms →Imperial guidance →

Funding for
a master’s degree.

These schemes support eligible taught master’s courses. They do not fund a PhD place in the lab.

UK / ELIGIBLE COMMONWEALTH COUNTRIES

Commonwealth Master’s Scholarships

Funding
Full approved tuition fees, a monthly living allowance and approved return airfare for the supported full-time taught master’s programme.
Who can apply
Eligible citizens or refugees and permanent residents of listed low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries. Academic, financial-need and development-impact conditions apply.
Application status
Closed for 2027/28 entry; the published deadline was 20 October 2026. Watch the official site for subsequent rounds.
How to proceed
Follow the provider’s nomination route and apply separately for an eligible course at Imperial.
Official scheme and funding terms →Imperial guidance →

UK / CHEVENING-ELIGIBLE COUNTRIES & TERRITORIES

Full Chevening Scholarships

Funding
Tuition fees, a monthly living allowance, approved travel, visa and other specified allowances for an eligible one-year master’s degree.
Who can apply
Applicants from eligible countries or territories who meet the academic, post-graduation work-experience and return-home requirements. Select an eligible Imperial course.
Application status
The current scholarship application round is closed at the date of this review. Check your country’s official award page for the next round.
Full-funding restriction
Only full awards for eligible non-MBA courses are included here. MBA awards and any award with a tuition cap leaving a fee shortfall are excluded. Confirm that the award covers your chosen course’s entire tuition fee.
