NATIONAL PROGRAMME / INDONESIA

LPDP — full-funding overseas awards

Master’s / PhD

Who it is for
Indonesian citizens meeting the chosen LPDP track’s requirements.
Funding coverage
Approved tuition and education costs, monthly living support, travel, visa and insurance under the full-funding route.
Funded duration
The published STEM Industri Strategis route funds up to 24 months for a master’s and 48 months for a doctorate.
Using it for study at Imperial
Use the overseas full-funding track and obtain approval for Imperial, the course and research field. STEM priorities include AI, food security, energy, materials and maritime research.
Important conditions
Return-and-contribution commitments apply. Partial-funding and co-funding variants are excluded from this shortlist.
Application position at review
The cited 2026 STEM round closed on 31 July 2026. Check LPDP for subsequent calls.

NATIONAL PROGRAMME / PHILIPPINES

DOST–SEI Foreign Graduate Scholarships

Master’s / PhD

Who it is for
Eligible Filipino applicants in specialised priority science and technology fields; sending-institution support is required.
Funding coverage
Actual tuition and other school fees, living allowance, travel, insurance and specified academic allowances.
Funded duration
The published brochure states up to 24 months for an MS and 36 months for a PhD. Confirm the current award duration.
Using it for study at Imperial
A potential route for approved overseas science and engineering study. The project, institution, budget and re-entry plan require DOST approval.
Important conditions
Return service in the Philippines is required. A 36-month award must not be treated as covering a longer PhD without an agreed plan for the remaining period.
Application position at review
Programme route verified; consult DOST–SEI for the applicable intake, priority fields and current terms.

NATIONAL PROGRAMME / INDIA

National Overseas Scholarship (NOS)

Master’s / PhD

Who it is for
Low-income Indian applicants in the scheme’s specified categories: Scheduled Castes; De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes; landless agricultural labourers; and traditional artisans.
Funding coverage
Tuition charged by the university, plus the scheme’s maintenance and other approved allowances. It is not a general scholarship for all Indian nationals.
Funded duration
Published scheme terms support master’s and PhD study within their respective maximum award periods; confirm the current UK terms and full course budget.
Using it for study at Imperial
Overseas postgraduate study, subject to current university-ranking, offer, income, age and selection rules.
Important conditions
Apply through the official NOS system. The Indian Mission handles approved payments; obtain confirmation of the fee award for the specific Imperial programme.
Application position at review
The Ministry publishes 2026/27 scheme guidance. Check the official portal for the next selection window.

NATIONAL RESEARCH PROGRAMME / SINGAPORE

A*STAR National Science Scholarship (PhD)

PhD

Who it is for
Singapore citizens, or eligible applicants committed to taking citizenship. Non-citizens must obtain Singapore permanent residence before study and convert to citizenship during the PhD.
Funding coverage
Full tuition, overseas living and sustenance allowances, travel and specified allowances for up to five years.
Funded duration
Up to five years of support under the current published scheme.
Using it for study at Imperial
An approved PhD at a leading overseas institution. The research must fit A*STAR’s priorities and approval process.
Important conditions
Normally includes a pre-PhD research attachment and a return-service bond. A*STAR states that lab consumables and research fees are not funded; these need a separate research budget.
Application position at review
The published annual windows end on 1 April and 1 October. Confirm the next call directly.

NATIONAL PROGRAMME / CHILE

ANID Becas Chile — Doctorado en el Extranjero

PhD

Who it is for
Chilean nationals and eligible foreign nationals with permanent residence in Chile who meet the doctoral competition’s academic requirements.
Funding coverage
Programme tuition and registration costs after any institutional discounts, with maintenance and other benefits governed by the overseas doctoral award.
Funded duration
Coverage applies during the awarded scholarship period. Confirm the duration and benefits in the current overseas doctoral competition.
Using it for study at Imperial
Doctoral study outside Chile in an eligible programme. Use the overseas award, not ANID’s domestic doctoral scholarship.
Important conditions
Admission, academic-merit, return and contribution requirements apply. Non-academic university services and costs outside the award period are excluded.
Application position at review
The published 2026 competition closed on 19 May 2026; check ANID for the next overseas doctoral call.

NATIONAL PROGRAMME / THAILAND

Royal Thai Government Science & Technology Scholarships

Master’s / PhD / combined routes

Who it is for
Thai applicants qualifying under the relevant general-public or public-sector call and priority research field.
Funding coverage
Full academic fees for the approved award period, a living allowance, insurance and overseas travel; specified research support is also described.
Funded duration
The published overview gives 3–4 years for standalone PhDs and 1–2 years for master’s study; the individual call controls.
Using it for study at Imperial
Overseas tracks include the UK. The university, field, degree and sponsoring agency must match the allocation.
Important conditions
A signed return-to-service obligation applies, with repayment consequences for breach. Check the specific NSTDA/OCSC call rather than assuming any subject is eligible.
Application position at review
Programme verified; published calendar windows are indicative and must be checked against the current official announcement.

MULTILATERAL PROGRAMME / ELIGIBLE ISDB MEMBER COUNTRIES

IsDB Merit Scholarship Programme for High Technology

PhD

Who it is for
Eligible researchers from IsDB member countries, nominated by their home educational or research institution. Relevant qualifications, research experience and publications are required.
Funding coverage
University tuition paid directly, a living stipend, insurance and specified travel and academic allowances.
Funded duration
The published MSP booklet describes 36 months of PhD support.
Using it for study at Imperial
Relevant fields include water resources, environmental technology, agriculture, materials and computing. Obtain written IsDB approval for Imperial as host before relying on this route; host eligibility is restricted.
Important conditions
Institutional nomination and return service apply. Visa/residence costs and some research expenses are excluded; plan these separately.
Application position at review
Possible route requiring host approval. Follow the current MSP booklet and call, not the rules of a different IsDB programme.

FOUNDATION PROGRAMME / SYRIA, JORDAN, LEBANON AND PALESTINE

Saïd Foundation Scholarships

Master’s only

Who it is for
Eligible Syrian, Jordanian, Lebanese and Palestinian nationals meeting residency, academic and leadership requirements. Published exceptions apply to displaced applicants.
Funding coverage
Full tuition and maintenance, plus specified travel, visa, healthcare and arrival costs for the supported course.
Funded duration
Principally one-year UK master’s study. This is not a PhD-funding route.
Using it for study at Imperial
Apply for an eligible UK course at a partner university, or justify an exceptional non-partner choice as the Foundation allows. Confirm the particular Imperial course’s eligibility.
Important conditions
A commitment to contribute to the home country or region applies. Family support is not included.
Application position at review
The Foundation states that its 2027/28 application round is closed. Follow its timetable for subsequent rounds.

FOUNDATION PROGRAMME / SPAIN AND PORTUGAL

“la Caixa” Foundation Postgraduate Fellowships Abroad

Master’s route — 9 to 24 months

Who it is for
Spanish or Portuguese nationals who completed the qualifying undergraduate studies in Spain or Portugal respectively, within the call’s specified dates.
Funding coverage
University tuition without a fee cap, a monthly allowance, and specified travel, insurance, visa and application-cost support.
Funded duration
9–24 months. Listed here for master’s courses completed within that period; a two-year fellowship is not full funding for a longer PhD.
Using it for study at Imperial
Eligible overseas postgraduate study includes UK institutions. MBA programmes are excluded.
Important conditions
Degree, language, mobility, start-date and academic-progress rules apply. Use the Postgraduate Abroad scheme, not the Spain/Portugal-based INPhINIT route.
Application position at review
The 2027 call is open until 21 January 2027 at 14:00 mainland-Spain time, according to the official page.

NATIONAL / BILATERAL PROGRAMME / UNITED STATES

Marshall Scholarships

Master’s / approved doctoral pathway

Who it is for
US citizens with an eligible US undergraduate degree, at least a 3.7 GPA and the scheme’s graduation-date and previous-UK-study requirements.
Funding coverage
University fees, living costs, travel and specified study allowances.
Funded duration
Master’s pathways fund one or two years. The current eligibility page describes three guaranteed years for direct-entry PhD candidates, subject to progress.
Using it for study at Imperial
Eligible UK degree study can be undertaken at Imperial. Confirm the exact pathway and programme approval.
Important conditions
US institutional endorsement is required. A master’s-then-PhD pathway does not automatically fund the whole doctorate; resolve any period beyond the guaranteed award before applying.
Application position at review
2027 applications are closed. The provider says applications for 2028 open in summer 2027.

03 / BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

A credible funding plan
supports a strong idea.

In your first email, name the scheme you intend to pursue and its relevant deadline, alongside the documents requested by the lab.

  1. Confirm your nationality or residency eligibility and that the exact Imperial programme and subject can be approved.
  2. Check the overseas tuition rate, compulsory academic fees, living support, funding duration and any separate lab or fieldwork costs.
  3. Read nomination, employer support, return-service and repayment-on-breach conditions before committing.
  4. Attach your current CV, transcripts and two-page personal statement when contacting the lab.
