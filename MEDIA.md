# Supplied research media

The slideshow displays the owner's seventeen supplied images, resized/compressed for browser use without generative alteration. Scientific/thermal imagery retains its original colours and aspect ratio, with centred cropping to fill the animation frame. The logo is 87.2% larger than its original size (successive increases of 30%, 20% and 20%), constrained to fit the available space. The sequence is decorative and does not introduce scientific claims.

Images advance every four seconds with crossfade transitions and a permanent group-logo overlay at 40% transparency (60% opacity). A dropdown below the animation lets visitors select an image and pause there, or choose Automatic sequence to resume. Every image has a descriptive text overlay matching its selector title; slides are not clickable. Animation stops while the page is hidden and for reduced-motion preferences. The original white background of the logo blends into the photograph through CSS multiply blending.

The provided 9.8-second Antarctica drone movie is included as a web-optimised H.264 MP4 with its original portrait orientation and optional audio. It starts muted, with native controls, a still poster and no autoplay. The original source MOV is not duplicated in the package; keep the uploaded original separately if you need full-quality video.

Media files are editorial assets and are never changed by the daily publication/PhD updater. To replace a photograph, edit the corresponding slide in `web/index.html` and its file in `web/assets/fieldwork/`. The animation fills its frame through `object-fit: cover`, allowing zoom/cropping.

Six additional supplied figures are interspersed through the sequence: ocean-model comparisons, thermal infrared and ocean tracers, global ocean REE observations, water-mass formation, reef-island mapping, and seasonal island responses.
